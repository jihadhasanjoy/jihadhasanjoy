Medicine Reminder – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the Medicine Reminder app (package com.dropwise.medicinereminder, "the app") handles information. The app is made by Jihad Hasan Joy, an independent developer ("I", "me"). If you have a question, write to jihadhasanjoy@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your medicines, family members, meal times and dose history are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to me or to anyone else.
  • The app uses Google services for ads (AdMob), anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and announcements (Firebase Cloud Messaging). These are described below.
  • I do not sell your data.

1. Information that stays on your device

To plan your reminders, the app keeps your name, the names of family members you add, your wake, sleep and meal times, your medicines (name, form, dose, times, days, start and end dates, pill count and refill level), the doses you take, skip or miss (the last 90 days), and your settings (theme, language, time format, reminder options). All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

If you turn on fingerprint or face confirmation, the check is done by your phone's operating system. The app only receives "confirmed" or "not confirmed"; it never sees your fingerprint or face.

2. Information collected by services the app uses

Service What it collects Why
Google AdMob Advertising ID, device and app information, IP address, and how you interact with ads. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first, and shows only non-personalised ads if you don't agree.
Firebase Analytics App usage events (for example "finished setup", "dose taken", "reminder snoozed"), with simple details such as how many doses were answered or the medicine form (tablet, syrup); device model, OS version, language, country and an app instance ID. Never the names of your medicines or people, your dose times or your health. To understand which features are used and improve the app.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs and diagnostics: device model, OS version, app version and the state of the app when it crashed. To find and fix bugs.
Firebase Cloud Messaging A push token for your installation. To send occasional announcements to everyone who uses the app (for example about a new version).

These services are provided by Google, which handles the data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. Feedback you choose to send

Settings has a "Send feedback" form. Only if you send it, the app sends your message, the name you type, your email address (optional, only if you want a reply) and, unless you untick it, basic device information (platform, OS version, phone brand and model, app language). It is stored in Google Firebase Firestore and emailed to me through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) via a page on my website. I use it only to read and answer your feedback.

4. Content the app downloads

The app downloads translations, settings and Medicine Guide articles from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when the app downloads these files. No personal information is sent with these requests.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: for dose reminders, refill alerts and the morning plan.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive at the planned minute.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for reminder alerts.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on fingerprint or face confirmation.
  • Internet: for ads, the services above and downloads.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

6. Sharing

I do not sell or rent your information. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google and FormSubmit) so they can provide their service, or if the law requires it.

7. Security and retention

All data the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your medicines and dose history stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app; dose records older than 90 days are removed on their own. Feedback messages are kept as long as needed to handle them; you can ask me to delete yours at any time. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's retention settings.

8. Your choices

  • Edit or delete your medicines, family members and history in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Ask me for a copy of, or to delete, any feedback you sent: jihadhasanjoy@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is meant for adults, including adults who manage medicines for their children. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent me information, contact me and I will delete it.

10. Health information

Medicine Reminder is a reminder tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions. The medicine and dose information you enter stays on your phone and is not used for ads or analytics.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
